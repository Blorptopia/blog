Functionality

ATS allows you to track

  • Which jobs you have applied to
  • When you applied to each job
  • How you found out about a job
  • What gave you the referral to that job
  • The interviews you attended

ATS also integrates with both Finn and NAV's arbeidsplassen to allow you to lookup an application by their post page.

History

Previously I was manually tracking this in a markdown document, however with time it became harder to track when things happened in the interview process. I eventually moved over to manually tracking this in a postgres database with a few views, however this wasn't that practical and I had the tendence to forget the where clause when updating and not notice before committing the transaction.
